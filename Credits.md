# Musician Forge credits

Musician Forge is an accessible digital audio workstation for Windows, made by
Russell Hinderman and built together with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.

## AI-assisted engineering

Musician Forge was made with AI-assisted engineering. Russell Hinderman designed it:
what it does, how it sounds and behaves, and its accessibility rules. He tested it by hand
on Windows with NVDA. Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, wrote the program code, the
tests and the documentation under his direction. Every commit in its history says so with a
Co-Authored-By: Claude line.

Musician Forge itself is free software under the MIT License (see the LICENSE file). The projects below keep
their own licenses.

Musician Forge stands on the work of these projects. Thank you to everyone behind them.

## Sounds

**GeneralUser GS 2.0.3**, the SoundFont that gives Musician Forge its 287
instruments and 13 drum kits, by S. Christian Collins.

- Website: <http://www.schristiancollins.com>
- License: free to use without restriction for your own music, private or
  commercial, and free to use in software projects. The full license is
  installed next to the SoundFont, in
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MusicianForge\soundfonts\GeneralUser-GS-License.txt`.

**FSBS Electric Guitar Distorted 1 and 2**, two SoundFonts of a Fender guitar
recorded through an amplifier and effects, by FreePats. Hammer, the guitar
rhythm player, plays them, and they can be played by hand on the musical keyboard.

- Website: <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ElectricGuitar/distorted-electric-guitar.html>
- Source: <https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-dist1> and
  <https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-dist2>
- License: CC0 1.0 (public domain). No credit is required; thank you anyway.

## Instrument engine

**FluidSynth 2.5.7**, the software synthesizer that plays the SoundFont.

- Website: <https://www.fluidsynth.org>
- License: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 (LGPL 2.1).
  Musician Forge uses the unmodified FluidSynth library from its official releases: the Windows download
  built with GLib (`fluidsynth-v2.5.7-win10-x64-glib.zip`). That download also contains GLib and
  libintl (LGPL 2.1 or later), libsndfile (LGPL 2.1) and SDL3 (zlib license), which the installer
  puts next to FluidSynth.
- Why not 2.6: the Windows downloads of FluidSynth 2.6 crash as soon as tracks have their own sound
  outputs (a pair of brackets is missing in FluidSynth's `FLUID_DECLARE_VLA`; found on 8 October 2026).
  Musician Forge refuses those builds and says how to replace them.

## Musician Forge's own sounds

The metronome clicks are generated by Musician Forge itself. The Forge Dubstep Kit
and the Forge 808 Sub (since version 0.4), and the Forge House Kit and Forge Trap
Kit (since version 0.8) are Musician Forge's own too, made from scratch inside the
program, so they carry no outside license.

## Methods

Finding the hits in a recording (for rhythm players that follow it) uses spectral
flux with a maximum filter, after S. Böck and G. Widmer, "Maximum filter vibrato
suppression for onset detection", DAFx 2013.
